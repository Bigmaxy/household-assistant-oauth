Household Assistant

A private, non-commercial personal assistant integration.

This site explains how a privately operated household assistant connects, with authorisation, to Google services. It is not a publicly accessible application, and access to the assistant is limited to its authorised users.

What the integration does

  • Google Tasks: view and manage household tasks and lists on request.
  • Google Calendar: read events and create or update appointments and reminders on request.
  • Gmail: access authorised messages to answer requests and prepare draft emails for user review. Outbound email is not sent automatically in the ordinary conversational workflow.

Why Google access is requested

Google permissions enable the functions above for accounts whose owners grant OAuth consent. Access is used to fulfil authorised requests, not to provide a general-purpose service to the public.
