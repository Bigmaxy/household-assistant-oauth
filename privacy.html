Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy describes the Google account data used by Household Assistant, a privately operated, non-commercial household assistant. This informational website is separate from the private assistant application.

Google data accessed

With the account holder's OAuth permission, the assistant can access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar events, and Google Tasks lists and entries. The actual access is governed by the permissions granted to the Google OAuth application.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to authorised household requests, including retrieving tasks and appointments, creating or updating requested tasks and events, summarising relevant messages, and preparing Gmail drafts for review. The integration is not designed for advertising, sale of user data, or public user profiling.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in the private assistant environment rather than on this public website. Assistant conversations and operational logs may contain information submitted or returned during requested operations. Access to the private system is restricted to authorised users. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

AI processing and third-party services

Requested data may be processed by the assistant's configured AI models and integrations to perform the requested task. Depending on the current configuration, processing may occur locally or through third-party services. Any use of external AI services with Google data must comply with Google's applicable API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Google data is not sold or used for advertising. It is disclosed to services only as necessary for an authorised task, such as the Google APIs and any AI or integration provider configured to perform that task. This website does not receive Google OAuth tokens or Google account content.

Retention and control

Google account holders can revoke the app's Google access in their Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorised API access but does not necessarily erase previously generated local notes, drafts or operational records. Removal of locally retained data is handled by the operator of the private assistant.

Public website

This static site has no user registration, contact forms, advertising scripts or site-operated analytics. Its hosting provider may process standard connection and security logs under its own policies.

Questions and updates

This application is private and not available for public registration. The policy will be updated if the assistant's data handling materially changes. Authorised users can contact the assistant operator through their existing private communication channel.
