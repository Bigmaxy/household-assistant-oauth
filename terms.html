Terms of use

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Household Assistant is a private, non-commercial personal assistant integration. This website provides information about the integration and does not provide public access to the assistant.

Authorised use

The integration is intended only for approved household users who have been granted access by its operator. Google account access requires the relevant account holder's authorisation.

Functionality

The assistant may retrieve information and carry out requested actions through connected services, subject to the permissions granted and the operator's configured approval controls. Users should review important outputs, including calendar changes, tasks and email drafts, before relying on them.

Availability

The assistant is supplied for private convenience without any commitment to continuous availability or error-free responses. Its operator may change or discontinue the integration.

Privacy and Google services

Use of Google features is subject to Google's applicable terms and the Household Assistant privacy policy. An account holder may revoke Google OAuth access through their Google Account settings.

Public access

Publication of this informational website does not grant anyone the right or ability to use the privately hosted assistant.
